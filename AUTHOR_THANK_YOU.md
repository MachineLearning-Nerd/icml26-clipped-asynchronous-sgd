# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Samuel Erickson and Mikael Johansson for developing a clear framework for studying clipping, staleness, heavy-tailed noise, and heterogeneous asynchronous optimization.

The paper’s explicit assumptions and separate theoretical and empirical claims make it possible to audit not only supporting mechanisms but also novelty scope, comparator choices, and missing historical protocol fields. This repository is an independent reproduction and audit record; its verdicts do not represent author review or endorsement.
