# Claim 6 falsification limitations

The rendered Figure 4 PDFs cannot recover exact per-seed raw curves. The
author code and omitted protocol fields are unavailable. The independent
reconstruction therefore cannot become an assumption-satisfying
counterexample to the paper's historical observation, regardless of how
substantive its discrepancy is.

The concrete unblockers are the author implementation, original raw curves and
seed identifiers, and the missing architecture/data/scheduler/cadence/
aggregation specification.
