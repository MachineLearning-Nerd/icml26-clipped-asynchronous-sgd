# Claim 6 mandatory falsification method

This route does not rerun training. It hash-pins the completed three-seed
validation and evaluates whether that discrepancy is a valid counterexample
to the exact source claim.

The eligibility classifier requires all material protocol fields to be
matched, plus either the same historical realization or a universally
quantified target. A contradiction is necessary but not sufficient. Two
positive controls prove the classifier accepts a fully matched universal
counterexample and an original-raw-data contradiction. A negative control
proves that a material assumption mismatch prevents falsification.
